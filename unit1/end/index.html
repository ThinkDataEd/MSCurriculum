    Unit 1 Investigative Report: Solving Your Own Case

    Unit 1 Investigative Report: Solving Your Own Case

    Mission Objective

    To demonstrate mastery of Unit 1 concepts by conducting a complete data investigation using the Data Cycle, analyzing data with CODAP, and presenting clear, evidence-based conclusions.

    Mission Background

    Congratulations, Data Detectives!

    You have successfully completed your initial training! You have mastered the art of identifying data types, asking statistical questions, and analyzing numerical data's shape, center, and spread using both manual techniques and our powerful digital toolkit, CODAP.

    Now, it's time for your Final Mission as a lead Data Detective! You will choose a “case” (a dataset) to investigate and formulate a relevant statistical question. You will then analyze the data using CODAP and present your findings in a comprehensive “Case Report.”

    This is your chance to show off all your detective skills!

    The Case Files (Data Options)

    You will choose ONE of the following datasets for your investigation. All of these are pre-loaded in CODAP.

    1. Roller Coasters: Contains data about roller coasters (ex. Height, Speed, Type, Material, etc.).

    2. Mammals: Contains data about various animals (ex. Sleep, Life Span, Diet, Type, etc.).

    3. Granola Bars: Contains nutritional data about different brands of granola bars (ex. Calories, Carbs, Fiber, Sugar, etc.).

    Mission Steps (The Data Cycle in Action)

    Your Case Report will follow the phases of the Data Cycle. You will need to provide evidence for each step.

    Phase I:
    Pose a Statistical Question
    • Choose one statistical question that can be answered using your chosen dataset.
    • Your question must involve at least one numerical variable.
    • Your question can involve a categorical variable if you want to explore relationships between groups.
    Phase II:
    Consider the Data
    • Identify the dataset you are using.
    • Briefly describe the source of the data. Is it primary data you collected, or secondary data collected by someone else?
    • Explain what information is provided in the dataset.
      • How many observations are there?
      • How many variables were reported?
    Phase III:
    Analyze the Data (Using CODAP!)
    • Use CODAP to create at least TWO different graphs that help you answer your statistical question. These could be:
      • Dot plots
      • Histograms
      • Boxplots
    • For each numerical variable relevant to your question, you must:
      • Describe its shape (symmetric, skewed right, skewed left, bimodal).
      • Determine the most appropriate measure of center (mean or median) and justify your choice based on the shape.
      • Determine the most appropriate measure of spread (IQR or MAD) and justify your choice based on the shape.
      • Identify any unusual or extreme observations.
    • Take clear screenshots of your CODAP graphs to include in your report.
    Phase IV:
    Interpret the Results
    • Write a clear conclusion that directly answers your statistical question.
    • Use evidence from your analysis (your graphs, measures of center, measures of spread, and observations about shape/relationships) to support your answer.
    • Explain what story the data tells you about your chosen “case.”

    Mission Case Report (Submission Requirements)

    You will submit a digital Case Report (ex. a Google Doc, Google Slides, Powerpoint, etc.)

    Your report must include the following sections:

    1. Detective Name: (Your Name)

    2. Case File Title: (A creative and/or relevant title for your investigation)

    3. My Statistical Question: (from Phase I)

    4. My Data Source: (from Phase II)

    5. My Analysis & Evidence: (from Phase III)

      1. Graph 1 & Observations:

        1. Screenshot of your first CODAP graph.

        2. Description of the distribution’s shape, best center (with justification), best spread (with justification), and any unusual observations.

      2. Graph 2 & Observations:

        1. Screenshot of your second CODAP graph.

        2. Description of the distribution’s shape, best center (with justification), best spread (with justification), and any unusual observations.

      3. OPTIONAL: Additional graphs/observations if needed to answer your statistical question.

    6. My Interpretation & Conclusion: (from Phase IV)

      1. Your answer to the statistical question.

      2. How your analysis (graphs, measures of center/spread, shape) supports your answer.
