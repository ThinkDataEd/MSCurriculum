#Unit 1, Section 3: Wrapping Up the Case 

**<h1>Section 3: Wrapping Up the Case</h1>**

##<p style="background: #00869b; color: white; text-align: left; padding: 10px;">**Overarching Themes**</p>

This section advances students from graphical inspection to formal numerical summarization within the Analyze Data and Interpret Data phases of the Data Cycle. Students investigate how numerical measures describe a distribution’s center and spread, beginning with the mean as a “fair share” value and the Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) as average distance from the center. By examining datasets with extreme values, students discover the need for alternative measures of center and spread: the median and the Interquartile Range (IQR). Students build a foundational statistical decision framework for pairing the mean and MAD with symmetric distributions and the median and IQR with skewed distributions.
