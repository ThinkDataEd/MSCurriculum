##**Space Shuttle Challenger Data A**

Description: 7 Space Shuttle unsuccessful flights data with 10 variables (mission (shuttle mission number), designation (mission designation code), shuttle (space shuttle name), date (date of launch), temp (temperature at launch in °F), pressure (leak check pressure in psi), erosion (number of O-ring failures involving erosion), blow-by (number of O-ring failures invovling blow-by), damaged (number of O-rings that showed damage after flight), undamaged (number of O-rings that showed *no* damage after flight))

- [Download data as CSV](../data/U2_Challenger_Data_A.csv)

- [Open Data in CODAP](https://codap.concord.org/app/#shared=https%3A%2F%2Fcfm-shared.concord.org%2F1VOpvS5sTLMHW8XUgTji%2Ffile.json "https://codap.concord.org/app/#shared=https%3A%2F%2Fcfm-shared.concord.org%2F1VOpvS5sTLMHW8XUgTji%2Ffile.json"){:target="_blank"}
